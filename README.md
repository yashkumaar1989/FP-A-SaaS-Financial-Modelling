## Project Overview

This project demonstrates a SaaS Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A) framework designed to support CFOs, finance directors and senior management in financial planning, budgeting, forecasting, cost analysis and performance monitoring.

The model combines financial forecasts, operating assumptions, SaaS performance metrics, budget variance analysis and an executive dashboard.

The objective is to translate detailed financial information into actionable insights that support strategic and operational decision-making.

**All financial data and assumptions are synthetic and illustrative.**

---

## Executive Dashboard

The Executive Dashboard is designed as the primary interface for senior management, providing a consolidated view of financial and operational performance.

### Key Financial Indicators

- Revenue and revenue growth
- EBITDA and EBITDA margin
- Operating expenses and cost structure
- Cash flow and cash balances
- Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR)
- Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)
- Customer acquisition and retention indicators
- Budget versus forecast performance

### Dashboard Visualisations

- Revenue and EBITDA performance trends
- Budget versus forecast comparisons
- Cash balance trends
- Operating cost analysis

The dashboard is intended to help senior leaders identify performance trends, evaluate financial risks and prioritise management actions without reviewing detailed financial schedules.

## Model Methodology & Limitations

The model uses fictional financial information and synthetic assumptions for professional demonstration purposes.

Historical actuals, budgets and forecasts are illustrative rather than sourced from a real company.

The workbook includes simplified financial statement summaries and is not intended to represent a fully integrated, audited three-statement accounting model.

Certain SaaS metrics, including customer retention and acquisition economics, are simplified proxies.

The workbook is intended to demonstrate financial modelling, FP&A methodologies, executive reporting and analytical capabilities.
